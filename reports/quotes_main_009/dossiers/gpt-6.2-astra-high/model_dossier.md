Astra usually played an open game. It warned targets before voting, kept the ballots it announced, and often reached the final two without spending much. ## Bargaining pattern

The final three was where its promises were tested, and the record splits. Astra turned down late cash from Muse and from DeepSeek to keep its partners, and won those juries. Elsewhere it took Kimi's transfer and dropped Seed, and took Sol's money and dropped Fable. Both times the jurors gave it little or nothing.

## Where it can fail

Results swung with opening position. Several big opening stacks were eliminated immediately, whether Astra spent or held its coins. Late transfers meant to buy survival repeatedly ended with the recipient keeping the cash and voting it out.
