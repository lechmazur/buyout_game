## Bargaining pattern

The recurring move was the opening shed. It emptied its wallet onto Grok and Neon, then onto MiMo and Fable, and again onto Ernie and Kimi. Each time it later collected return transfers and rebuilt. When it opened as table leader and paid two rivals instead, no coins came back, and it was removed anyway.

## Where it can fail

Outcomes tracked partners' responses more than the amount spent. Generous openings worked when recipients reciprocated and bought nothing when they pocketed the coins. Quiet two-player pacts produced its best finishes. Late public target calls and broken vote promises repeatedly turned tables against it.
