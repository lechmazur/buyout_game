Claude Sonnet's results tracked its starting stack. From short or middle wallets, it voted with majorities, absorbed gifts and often reached the endgame. From the richest seat, or after publicly naming a stack to remove, it was usually voted out on the first ballot. In those games it kept its wallet and gained little else.

## Bargaining pattern

At the final three, it often paid a partner for a vote, and partners kept the coins and voted the other way. Step joined Qwen against it. DeepSeek sided with Grok after repeated payments. Grok later did the same. Earlier rescue offers, made when it was the opening target, were mostly refused or never executed.
